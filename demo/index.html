Compressed scale: every direction is real, but distances are squeezed (the Moon is drawn 10 Earth radii away, it is 60) and the Sun and planets are drawn larger than life. Switch to True scale to see how empty space is.

Put it in your page

One iframe, no script of your own. The address chooses what it shows: look, centre, graticule, and whether it turns by itself.

<iframe src="https://johnmorrisdotca.github.io/chikyu-earth/embed.html?look=thick&center=10,30"
        title="The Earth" width="420" height="420" loading="lazy"></iframe>
